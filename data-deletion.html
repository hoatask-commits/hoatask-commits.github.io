KcalMotion Dance – Data Deletion Request

Last Updated: August 2026

KcalMotion Dance allows users to request deletion of data associated with online features such as the Global Leaderboard and Firebase services.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your KcalMotion Dance data, please send an email to:

Email: dvnhadatvnn@gmail.com

Please use the following email subject:

KcalMotion Dance Data Deletion Request

In your message, please provide enough information to help us identify the relevant leaderboard record, such as your KcalMotion Dance nickname.

Request Data Deletion

Data That May Be Deleted

After we can reasonably identify the relevant record, we may delete applicable KcalMotion Dance data stored for the Global Leaderboard, including:

  • Leaderboard nickname
  • Country code used to display the country flag
  • Level
  • XP or leaderboard score
  • Leaderboard update information
  • Associated Firebase leaderboard record

Where technically applicable, associated anonymous Firebase information used specifically for the KcalMotion Dance leaderboard may also be removed as part of the deletion process.

Locally Stored Workout Data

Workout history and fitness information stored locally on the user's device can be removed using available deletion controls in KcalMotion Dance, by clearing the App's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the App.

Locally stored workout data is not automatically removed from the user's device by submitting an email request to us because that data is stored on the user's own device.

Camera Images and Videos

KcalMotion Dance does not store camera photos, videos, or camera frames in Firebase Realtime Database as part of its AI motion analysis. Camera frames used for motion analysis are processed on the device.

Data Retention

Leaderboard information may be retained until it is no longer necessary to provide the Global Leaderboard or until a valid deletion request is processed.

Certain limited information may be retained where necessary for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or technical purposes.

If information must be retained for one of these reasons, it will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for that purpose.

Processing Your Request

After receiving a valid deletion request, we will review the information provided and take reasonable steps to identify the relevant KcalMotion Dance leaderboard record.

If additional information is required to identify the relevant record, we may reply to the email address used to submit the request.
